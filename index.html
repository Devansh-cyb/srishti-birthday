TO MY BIRTHDAY GIRL

Dear Srishti,

Happy birthday, you. ♡

I know we’re still at the beginning of our story, and maybe that’s what makes this feeling so special. There’s so much we’re still getting to know about each other, so many little moments still waiting for us. But even in this beginning, you’ve already made a beautiful difference in my days.

You have this way of making me feel comfortable just by being you. You make me happy in ways that don’t need grand plans or perfect words, and the care you show me means more than I always know how to explain. With you, I feel like I can breathe, be myself, and let a day become a little lighter.

For your birthday, I hope life gives you back the gentleness you give to people. I hope you find a hundred reasons to smile, feel appreciated in the smallest moments, and always remember how worthy you are of kindness, happiness, and beautiful things.

I can’t promise that every day will be perfect, but I do hope I can keep adding little bits of happiness to your days—one laugh, one thoughtful message, one memory at a time. No pressure to make this story anything other than what feels right; I’m just genuinely glad that I get to know you.

So today, celebrate yourself. Your smile, your heart, your little ways of caring, and the person you are becoming. The world is lucky to have you in it—and I feel lucky that our paths crossed.

Happy birthday, Srishti. May this new year of your life be soft with you, generous to you, and full of moments that make your heart happy.

With a big smile, and lots of care ♡
